snake that knows · how the mind is wired

twenty-two hypotheses

The snake's beliefs do not depend on each other directly. They all pour into one number, and that number decides which of them is allowed to move next.

src/mind/beliefs.js · src/mind/mind.js · src/engine/game.js

It is a loop, not a tree

There is no edge from one belief to another anywhere in the code. What looks like a dependency tree is a feedback circuit with a single scalar in the middle.

Every belief holds a confidence from 0 to 1. Those confidences are summed against fixed weights into one value — knowledge — and each belief carries a gate, the knowledge level below which that belief cannot move at all, no matter how much evidence arrives. So beliefs reach each other only through the pooled total. Learning that food makes you longer raises the total, and the raised total is what unlocks the idea that you are being scored.

The circuit closes twice. Knowledge feeds back into the gates, and it also rewrites the rules of the world through applyTo — which brings three event types into existence that could not have happened earlier.

The ladder

All 22 hypotheses, placed at the exact knowledge level where each is first permitted to form. The rungs are not evenly spaced: eight of them are open before the meter reads 6%, then the gaps widen and stay near-linear all the way to the top.

What feeds what

Every increment in the system, grouped by the event that causes it. Note the rates: a move fires 8–13 times a second and pays in ten-thousandths, while a death pays in fifths. That ratio is what makes playstyle change the shape of the climb rather than just its speed.

The two rungs you can saw off

Because a belief moves only on evidence, a playstyle that never produces a given event leaves that rung permanently at zero. Two beliefs sit at the ends of this — and no amount of extra play recovers them.
